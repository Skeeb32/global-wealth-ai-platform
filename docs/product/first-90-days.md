# First 90 days

| Period | Outcome | Acceptance evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Days 1–30 | Select and design one pilot | Approved process inventory, business owner, baseline, metric contract, architecture decisions and acceptance criteria |
| Days 31–60 | Build calculation and reconciliation foundation | Synthetic ingestion-to-output run, reference results, validation, lineage, exception handling and repeatable checks |
| Days 61–90 | Prove a controlled pilot | Historical-case comparison, shadow-mode review, failure recovery, measured improvement, operational runbook and go/no-go decision |

Do not replace an authoritative process before business, security, and operational approvals. Q2 introduces the first production workflow and approved retrieval pilot; Q3 extends shared APIs and workflows; Q4 closes governance and support gaps and expands proven patterns. Delivery depends on access, staffing, approvals, and integration readiness.
